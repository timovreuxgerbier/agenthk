Shyft Open source on GitHub

Your screenshots, sorted while you sleep.

Shyft looks at your new screenshots on your computer and suggests what to keep, turn into text, or clear out. You decide. Nothing is deleted.

Download for Macyour computer Shyft-Tester.dmg · macOS 14 or newer Download for Windowsyour computer Shyft-Tester-Windows.zip · Windows 10 or 11
Free · uses made-up screenshots only · nothing is sent anywhere

Why you'd want it

Find things again

That flight time, that recipe, that reading list. Shyft names them and turns the useful ones into text.

Clear the clutter, safely

Copies and blank captures go to a To Be Deleted folder. You empty it yourself, whenever you like.

Private by design

Everything stays on your computer. Screenshots that look private get locked, and Shyft stops reading them.

Installing, step by step

Open the downloadDouble-click Shyft-Tester.dmg. A window opens with three numbered files and a READ ME.
Double-click "1 Install Shyft"Your Mac will say it "could not verify" it, because we're a student team, not a registered Apple developer. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. Once per file.
Double-click "2 Run the Shyft test"Two runs on made-up screenshots. Leave the window open; your Mac stays awake by itself.
Send your resultsA "Shyft test results" folder appears on your Desktop. Send it to Sphinx. Then "3 Remove Shyft" gives the 17 GB back.
Extract the downloadRight-click Shyft-Tester-Windows.zip › Extract All. The files don't work from inside the zip.
Double-click "1 Install Shyft"If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info › Run anyway. We're a student team, not a registered publisher. If it asks to install Python, click Yes.
Double-click "2 Run the Shyft test"A quick check first, with a time estimate. Then you choose whether to do the two full runs. Keep the lid open; it restarts Ollama.
Send your resultsA "Shyft test results" folder appears on your Desktop. Send it to Sphinx. Then "3 Remove Shyft" removes only what the kit brought.

How it works

Screenshots go to one folderTake them like always. To start, Shyft only looks in that folder.
Rick looks while you sleepOnce a week, on your computer, he suggests what to do with each one.
You decideApprove or change each suggestion. At first, nothing moves without your OK.
