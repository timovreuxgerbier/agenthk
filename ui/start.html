Shyft Open source on GitHub

Your screenshots, sorted while you sleep.

Shyft looks at your new screenshots on your Mac and suggests what to keep, turn into text, or clear out. You decide. Nothing is deleted.

Download the tester kit for Mac Shyft-Tester.dmg · free · uses made-up screenshots only

Why you'd want it

Find things again

That flight time, that recipe, that reading list. Shyft names them and turns the useful ones into text.

Clear the clutter, safely

Copies and blank captures go to a To Be Deleted folder. You empty it yourself, whenever you like.

Private by design

Everything stays on your Mac. Screenshots that look private get locked, and Shyft stops reading them.

What you need

  • A Mac or a PCmacOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer · Windows 10 (22H2) or 11
  • Memoryat least 8 GB · 16 GB is better
  • Free spaceabout 17 GB
  • The Ollama appfree · the installer gets it for you
  • Time30 to 90 minutes, mostly waiting

Installing, step by step

Open the downloadDouble-click Shyft-Tester.dmg. A window opens with three numbered files and a READ ME.
Double-click "1 Install Shyft"Your Mac will say it "could not verify" it, because we're a student team, not a registered Apple developer. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. Once per file.
Double-click "2 Run the Shyft test"Two runs on made-up screenshots. Leave the window open; your Mac stays awake by itself.
Send your resultsA "Shyft test results" folder appears on your Desktop. Send it to Sphinx. Then "3 Remove Shyft" gives the 17 GB back.
Extract the downloadRight-click Shyft-Tester-Windows.zip › Extract All. The files don't work from inside the zip.
Double-click "1 Install Shyft"If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info › Run anyway. We're a student team, not a registered publisher. If it asks to install Python or git, click Yes.

How it works

Screenshots go to one folderTake them like always. Shyft only looks in that folder.
Rick looks while you sleepOnce a week, on your Mac, he suggests what to do with each one.
You decideApprove or change each suggestion. Only then does anything move.
