Shyft Open source on GitHub

Your screenshots, sorted while you sleep.

Shyft looks at your new screenshots on your Mac and suggests what to keep, turn into text, or clear out. You decide. Nothing is deleted.

Download for Mac Free · open source · beta

Why you'd want it

Find things again

That flight time, that recipe, that reading list. Shyft names them and turns the useful ones into text.

Clear the clutter, safely

Copies and blank captures go to a To Be Deleted folder. You empty it yourself, whenever you like.

Private by design

Everything stays on your Mac. Screenshots that look private get locked, and Shyft stops reading them.

What you need

  • A Mac[which Macs: from Mac test]
  • Free spaceabout 11 GB
  • The Ollama appfree · Shyft helps you get it
  • Time[download time: from Mac test] + about 5 min setup

How it works

Screenshots go to one folderTake them like always. Shyft only looks in that folder.
Rick looks while you sleepOnce a week, on your Mac, he suggests what to do with each one.
You decideApprove or change each suggestion. Only then does anything move.
